B

Ball position

Play the ball further back in your stance than you'd think. Once in posture, swing the club to see where it's hitting the ground.

Setup GuideContact
I

Incline at the hips

Hinge the hips, stand tall, no hunching. Stay in it.

Posture CheckBalance
R

Realign shoulders

Realign both the shoulders and the hips to the target line, then hold the tilt. Keep your armpits pinned, like the towel drill.

Swing PathTakeaway Plane
D

Deliberate tempo

Keep the tempo deliberate, never rushed. The clubhead's momentum pulls on your hands, and that pull lifts the shoulders up along their plane. At the top, don't pull the club down. Let the club and hands fall under their own weight, and that drop is what shallows the club.

Downswing SequenceDrop Arms
I

Impact

At impact, stay up and forward with the belt buckle moving toward the target, so you don't hit behind the ball. Keep your hands quiet.

Quiet HandsStop Hit
E

End with a good finish

Stay true to the shoulder axis and the target line to avoid pulls, cuts, and hooks. Return naturally to the finish with no jerky motions, and rehearse it in practice swings from your starting position.
